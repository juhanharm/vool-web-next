# Brief for the new www.vool.com

Goal: the site has one job, to turn visitors into leads and orders. Every page ends in one clear next step.

Audiences, in priority order: (1) apartment associations and property managers, (2) resellers and installers, (3) businesses, fleets and public charging hosts, (4) home owners.
